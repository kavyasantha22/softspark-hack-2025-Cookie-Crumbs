## 🐛 **Troubleshooting**

### **No Notifications?**
1. Check: Profile → Notifications → Green checkmark?
2. iOS Settings → SparkPlay → Allow Notifications
3. Turn OFF "Do Not Disturb"
4. Use "Test Notification" to verify

### **Demo Timing Off?**
1. Use "Demo: Quick Spark" instead of regular sparks
2. Focus on immediate notifications
3. Show "Pending Notifications" to prove scheduling works

### **App Crashes?**
1. Check all environment objects are injected
2. Restart app
3. Re-enable notification permissions
